Learn melody: notation and guitar TAB
      

        
How to read it: use the six TAB lines and play the numbered fret aligned with each note above. 0 means an open string. These standard-tuning fingerings sound one octave below the fiddle, which keeps the melody in a comfortable guitar position.

    Chords come from the chord symbols on each tune's sheet music where it has them, mapped to your capo shapes. Otherwise they come from your own chart, and a highlighted tag on the song says when the chords or timing are estimated or suggested. Melodies play from the notation saved in your songbook's Learn Melody sections, at fiddle pitch, wherever a tune has one. Two songs have no backing: Água de Beber and How Insensitive are bossa nova chord-melody arrangements that don't fit a boom-chick vamp, and How Insensitive's sheet isn't saved in the songbook. Their songbook notes are still here, under Songbook only in the song list.
